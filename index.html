Physics describer

The train rises to the selected starting height, then gravity converts gravitational potential energy into kinetic energy.

v = sqrt(2g(h0 - h))

Speed: height is converted to speed using no-friction conservation of energy.

G-force: the displayed value is 1 g from gravity plus the normal acceleration around track curvature. A valley can raise g-force; a crest can lower it.

Failure: a crest above the available starting energy is unreachable. The car stops or rolls back. It also fails above 4 g or below -1 g.
